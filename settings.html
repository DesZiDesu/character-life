Independent Theme Creator

Create a complete Header, Monologue, and Dialogue theme from a neutral blank canvas. Independent themes do not use a built-in theme as their visual foundation.

& means the selected component. Independent themes first neutralize Character Life's built-in visual styling, then your scoped Header, Monologue, and Dialogue CSS defines the complete appearance. Flat rules can style children such as .cl-chat-header-core, .cl-chat-portrait, .cl-chat-label, and .cl-chat-content.

Press New blank theme to start from an independent canvas.
